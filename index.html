READY, SET, GROW

Let’s make little sums.

  1. Pick addition, subtraction, or a mix of both.
  2. Count the dots, or switch to numbers.
  3. Type your answer or use the number buttons. Correct answers are checked automatically. Press Enter to check an unfinished answer.
  4. A right answer earns a happy little win! If it’s not quite right, listen to the question again and give it another try. After 3 wrong attempts on the same question, see the correct answer for 3 seconds before the next question appears.
  5. Milo starts 10 steps from the gift and 3 steps from the creature. A right answer moves him one step toward a random gift; a wrong answer moves him one step toward a hungry creature.
  6. Reach Surprise Summit to collect a cake, car, house, rocket, or teddy bear. Reach Munchy Meadow and a silly creature gobbles Milo up! Choose “Let’s go again” for a fresh adventure.

Choose Let’s play to turn on spoken questions. Listen for Milo’s little footsteps, a cheerful prize fanfare, and a silly creature gulp! The sound button turns voices and all sound effects on or off. There’s no timer, so take your time.
