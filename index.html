 READY, SET, GROW

Let’s make little sums.

  1. Pick addition, subtraction, or a mix of both.
  2. Count the dots, or switch to numbers.
  3. Type your answer or use the number buttons. Correct answers are checked automatically. Press Enter to check an unfinished answer.
  4. The sparkle difficulty adapts each new question to your answers and your own pace, within 50. Star options keep a fixed range. Open Learning progress from the player menu to see skills, number patterns and practice suggestions.
  5. In Dots mode, a correct answer flips the dot cards into a number sentence. Listen to it once, then take 2 seconds to look before the next dot question appears.
  6. A right answer earns a happy little win! If it’s not quite right, listen to the question again and give it another try. After 3 wrong attempts on the same question, see the correct answer for 3 seconds before the next question appears.
  7. Milo starts 5 steps from the gift and 2 steps from the creature. Each adventure picks a new world and activity: climb stairs, scale walls, swim, ski, fly, and more. A right answer moves him one step toward a random gift; a wrong answer moves him one step toward a hungry creature.
  8. Reach the finish to collect one of 60 surprise prizes. Medium adventures earn Silver prizes; Hard adventures earn Golden prizes. Changing difficulty starts a fresh adventure. After the celebration, Milo automatically starts a new adventure. Go back to the snack stop and one of 60 silly creatures gobbles Milo up! A bouncy sad tune plays before a fresh adventure starts automatically.
  9. Create a player profile or log in from the top of the page to keep your prizes. Click your treasure box to see every kind you have collected, with matching treasures stacked together. Guest play keeps treasures only for this visit.

Choose Let’s play to turn on spoken questions. Listen for Milo’s little footsteps, a cheerful prize fanfare, and a silly creature gulp! The sound button turns voices and all sound effects on or off. There’s no timer, so take your time.
